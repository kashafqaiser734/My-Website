About the City

Zurich is the largest city of Switzerland. It lies beside beautiful Lake Zurich and the Limmat River, while the magnificent Swiss Alps can be admired from nearby viewpoints on clear days.

The city is famous for its clean streets, historic buildings and delicious chocolate. Zurich brings together nature, culture and modern life, making it a destination I would love to visit.

"Zurich is a place where old history and peaceful nature meet."
