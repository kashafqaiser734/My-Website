Get to Know the Dishes

Learn a little more about three traditional Swiss favourites.

Cheese Fondue
Melted cheese served in a communal pot, traditionally eaten by dipping small pieces of bread into the warm mixture.
Raclette
Melted raclette cheese scraped over potatoes and commonly served with pickles and other accompaniments.
Rosti
A crispy potato dish made from grated potatoes that are shaped and fried until golden.
